The short version

The positioning is sharp and the writing is disciplined. Every fee claim on the page is cited and dated, no fake artist data ships, and the fee chart has a screen-reader description written by someone who cared. That is rarer than it should be.

The two problems that cost you the most are both about a gap between promise and delivery. A music platform's homepage plays no music: the hero shows a mock storefront with [your album] in it. And the two loudest buttons say "start selling" and "claim your artist page", then scroll to a form that asks for an email and says someone will be in touch.

Under that sits a vocabulary problem. You told me the reader is a musician new to all of this. The word "sats" arrives in the first sentence under the headline, and "Nostr events signed by your key" is the opening line of the section addressed to artists. Fourteen articles that would fix this exist at /learn, linked only from the footer.

Already right — don't lose these

Every fee number is cited

The chart pulls its sources from a shared module with a checked date, so a claim can't ship without its citation. Keep that coupling.

The ASCII chart is accessible

Block characters are hidden from assistive tech and paired with a written description of the same comparison. Most teams skip this.

No fake artists

Bracketed placeholders instead of invented bands. Honest, and it reads as a template the visitor will fill. Worth keeping once real music is also on the page.

Contrast ratios are in the tokens

Muted text carries its measured ratio in a comment, and faint text is marked large-only. That is a system that will hold up as it grows.

If you only do four things

ONE

Put a real, playable storefront in the hero. You already ship the player and the artist pages.

TWO

Make the buttons tell the truth. "Join the artist beta" costs you nothing and loses no one at the form.

THREE

Write the hero in dollars. Introduce sats and Nostr once, lower down, each with one plain sentence.

FOUR

Promote Learn to the header and raise the base type size to 16px. Two small diffs, biggest effect per line changed.
