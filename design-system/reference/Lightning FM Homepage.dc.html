What makes us different?

Understand →

Artists keep 100% of their sales. This is because we're built using two open protocols: Nostr and Bitcoin's Lightning Network.

If you make music

Your page, your catalog and your audience stay yours. Nobody can suspend the account, change the terms, or hold the payout — because there is no account and no payout.

Technically: your catalog is published as Nostr events signed by your own key, so it can be read by any client and moved anywhere. What that means →

If you listen

Press play. No account, no signup, no meter running. When you want to own the file, one payment unlocks the download on the spot.

Your receipt is cryptographic, so you can re-download later without us remembering who you are.

If you build

Every purchase is one invoice, instant delivery and a verifiable receipt — a catalog a machine can buy from.

Stem licensing and agent APIs are next. The desktop app, artist node and MCP server are MIT. Read the spec →

From finished track to live listing

One desktop app does the whole job. No distributor, no review queue, no monthly fee.

Don't want to run anything? Sell through our hosted gate instead. We store and serve the files; buyers still pay your wallet directly. We check the payment settled and unlock the download. That is all we touch.
